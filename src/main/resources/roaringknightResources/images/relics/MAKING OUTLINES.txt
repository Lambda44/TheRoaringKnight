Open Relic Image in GIMP
Select the Magic Wand tool (the one that looks like a wand w/ a Star on the end)
Click on the transparency surrounding the image
CTRL+i to invert the selection
Right-click the canvas > Select > Grow
Increase border by 3 pixels
In bottom right corner, Shift-click on the New Layer button
Use bucket tool to fill selection with White
Export
