# The Roaring Knight

A custom character mod for Slay the Spire, which features:
75 new cards (+3 character-specific Colorless cards)
11 new character-specific Relics
3 new character-specific Potions

---

Gameplay
As a being of pure darkness, The Roaring Knight has access to power others would only dream of. Notably, expect a high level of access to your Exhaust pile and large amounts of card creation. Each archetype has been designed to be cross compatible with each other.
Stars
A colorless card akin to Shivs, dealing damage when played and when Exhausted.
Shadow
Shadow cards Exhaust themselves at the start of each combat. Bring them back to your hand with Warp cards.
Swoon
A debuff that primarily triggers its HP loss from each application of Swoon.
The Roaring
A high-risk, high-reward playstyle. If you play enough Titan cards, you can get access to powerful scaling. You just need to survive to get to that point...
